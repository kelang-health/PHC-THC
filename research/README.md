# OSM-PHC Research Data Structure

This folder contains public, non-personal research documentation and blank data schemas for the three draft studies planned from routine OSM-PHC work at Tok Hua Chang Public Health Center.

## Draft study tracks

- **R1 — Digital community health workflow:** develop and evaluate the digital workflow for organizing community health work.
- **R2 — NCD follow-up:** evaluate coverage and continuity of follow-up for people at risk of NCDs.
- **R3 — Home visits:** evaluate assignment and follow-up processes for home visits by the health team and village health volunteers.

These are working titles and variable proposals. Final definitions, populations, outcomes, time periods, and data sources must follow the approved research protocol and ethics review.

## Data boundary

GitHub stores only the codebook and blank column schemas. Do not commit participant-level records, clinical results, names, PID/HN, house identifiers, addresses, phone numbers, exact birth dates, coordinates, linkage keys, exports, or screenshots containing real records. Store authorized research data in the access-controlled research workspace, separately from this public code repository. Keep any re-identification key separately with restricted access, if the approved protocol requires one.

Before collection, confirm that each variable is available and defined consistently in the source system, minimize sensitive fields, obtain the required organizational and ethics approvals, and document extraction date, source version, missing-data handling, and corrections.
